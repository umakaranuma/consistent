Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at Fynux ("Company", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about our policy, or our practices with regards to your personal information, please contact us at support@fynux.com.

1. Information We Collect

VitaTrack is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal personal information. All health and fitness data (such as weight, hydration logs, and daily schedules) is stored locally on your device.

  • Device Access: We request notification permissions strictly to send you scheduled health reminders.
  • Local Storage: Your schedules, themes, and health logs are stored offline securely using local databases.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected strictly for app functionality:

  • To provide timely and accurate health reminders.
  • To calculate and present your Body Mass Index (BMI) and health trends locally.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

Currently, VitaTrack operates entirely offline. It does not integrate with third-party analytics or tracking frameworks (such as Firebase or Google Analytics), ensuring your data remains exclusively on your device.

4. Data Retention

Since your health data is stored locally on your device, it remains there until you decide to uninstall the app or clear the app data. We do not retain copies of your data on external servers.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time in order to reflect changes to our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date of this policy.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this policy, you may email us at support@fynux.com.
